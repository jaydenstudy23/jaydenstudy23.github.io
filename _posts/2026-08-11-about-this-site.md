Short first post to explain what this is.

<!--more-->

Roughly, three kinds of things will end up here:

- **Competitive programming write-ups.** Mostly Australian Informatics Olympiad
  problems, and not just the accepted solution.
- **Project notes.** Whatever I'm building, and the decisions inside it that
  weren't clear at the time.
- **Things I'm learning.** Some things are intentionally incompletely posted.
